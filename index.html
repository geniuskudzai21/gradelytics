FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Gradelytics is 100% free with no hidden fees, no trials, and no account required. Just open the dashboard and start using it.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no password. All your data is stored locally in your browser — we never see it.

How does the AI extraction work?

Upload a screenshot of your results table and our AI vision model reads it, extracts every module name, mark, and grade, and adds them to your dashboard automatically.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Everything stays in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server except the AI API calls for extraction and predictions. Your grades never leave your device for storage.

How accurate are the predictions?

AI predictions are based on your historical performance patterns. They're designed as guidance, not guarantees — use them as one input alongside your own judgement.

What if I add the wrong data?

You can edit or delete any module at any time. There's also a "Delete All" button if you want to start fresh.
